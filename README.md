## 🚀 Projects

### 🦾 GDSense — Autonomous Warehouse Patrol Robot
*Apr 2026 — Present · [Gradisen](https://gradisen.com)*

An autonomous patrol robot for small-to-medium warehouses that detects packaging damage and verifies SKUs **without infrastructure modifications**. Built on an **NVIDIA Jetson** edge-AI pipeline with **ROS**, integrating a YOLO-based vision model, RGB-D camera, and 2D LiDAR to navigate fixed routes and identify structural anomalies with **over 80% accuracy**.

`ROS` `NVIDIA Jetson` `YOLO` `LiDAR` `RGB-D` `Sensor Fusion` `Autonomous Navigation`

### 📦 NovaSense — 3D Inventory Counting System
*Dec 2025 — Apr 2026*

An advanced Edge AI system for warehouse inventory counting and volumetric estimation. Evolved from a YOLO bounding-box model into a sophisticated **2D-3D spatial fusion pipeline** combining **Grounding DINO**, **SAM**, and **MASt3R**. Engineered a custom point-cloud denoising engine and elevation map voxelization to accurately measure irregular cargo shapes.

`3D Reconstruction` `Point Cloud Processing` `Computer Vision` `Edge AI` `Spatial Fusion`

### 🪑 Sitting Director — Smart Posture-Correcting Chair
*Sep 2022 — May 2023*

A smart chair system using an **ESP-32**, FSRs, and infrared sensors to detect and correct poor posture in real time. Implemented on-device pose estimation via **TensorFlow Lite** and **Google MoveNet**, syncing data to a custom **Android app** via **Firebase** and **BLE**. Designed 3D-printed mechanical components for physical posture adjustment.

`IoT (ESP-32)` `TensorFlow Lite` `Android (Java)` `Firebase` `BLE` `Hardware Integration`

## 🔮 Looking Ahead — Future Aspirations

### 🏢 Gradisen — Building the Future of Warehouse Automation

My immediate focus is scaling **Gradisen** from a startup into a leading robotics company. The vision: autonomous warehouse systems that are **plug-and-play** — no infrastructure overhauls, no downtime. GDSense is just the beginning. I aim to build a full ecosystem of edge-AI powered robots that make warehouses smarter, safer, and more efficient.

### 🤖 Advancing Edge AI & Robotics

I want to push the boundary of what **on-device AI** can achieve. Moving beyond cloud-dependent models toward robust, real-time inference at the edge — making robots that can see, think, and act autonomously in dynamic environments. Goals include:

- **Multi-robot coordination** — fleets of autonomous robots that collaborate in real time
- **General-purpose warehouse perception** — one vision system that handles all inspection, counting, and anomaly detection tasks
- **Open-source contributions** — giving back to the ROS, computer vision, and edge AI communities

### 🎓 Completing BEng & Beyond

After completing my Computer Engineering degree at **HKUST** (expected 2028), I plan to continue bridging academia and industry — perhaps pursuing a research-based postgraduate degree while running Gradisen, contributing to the fields of **3D computer vision**, **robotic manipulation**, and **edge computing**.

### 💡 Lifelong Mission

At my core, I believe technology should be **practical, accessible, and impactful**. Whether it's a smart chair helping someone sit better, or a robot making warehouse workers' lives easier — I want to build things that **matter to real people**. My goal is to lead a technology company that ships products used by millions, while staying rooted in hands-on engineering.

### 🩺 Community & Service
